✨ What is murmur-youtube?

murmur-youtube is a simple, powerful dictation tool that turns your voice into text in real time. Whether you're writing an email, chatting with friends, or drafting a document, you just press a button, speak, and the words appear on your screen. It's that easy.

The magic? Everything runs directly on your device. No internet connection needed, no audio sent to servers, no privacy concerns. Your words never leave your computer. This makes it extremely fast, secure, and reliable.

Think of it as a smart voice assistant that focuses on one thing: getting your spoken words into text flawlessly. It's perfect for anyone who wants to type faster or has difficulty using a keyboard.

🔧 How to Use It

After installation, using murmur-youtube is child's play:

  1. Open the app – it will sit quietly in your system tray or menu bar.
  2. Hold the hotkey (default is Right Ctrl by default, but you can change it in settings).
  3. Speak naturally – separate sentences with a brief pause.
  4. Release the key – your text will be typed wherever your cursor is.

💡 Pro tip: Say "new line" to jump to the next line, and "period" or "comma" to add punctuation. The correction dictionary learns your style over time!
